Help

Examples of regular expression searches:

  • ^a → terms starting with “a”
  • ing\b → terms ending in “ing”
  • [aeiou]{2} → terms containing two vowels in a row
  • ^.{1,3}$ → terms of length 1–3
  • .*foo.*bar → terms containing “foo” then “bar”
  • ([пбтдкгцчшщфвсзжхмнрлй])\1 → terms a doubled letter
  • \p{M} → finds terms with a combining mark
  • \p{sc=Javanese} → finds terms using the Javanese script
